# UMAMUSUME AI KNOWLEDGE BASE

> Unified AI-readable knowledge extracted from Community Guides 1–4.
>
> Purpose: provide a compact, structured reference for AI analysis, run evaluation, and decision support.
>
> Source guides are treated as source material. Human-oriented prose, repetition, and unsupported personal opinion are excluded from the final knowledge unless they contain verifiable game information.

---

## 00. DOCUMENT RULES

### KNOWLEDGE_STATUS

- `FACT` — Directly supported game/mechanical information.
- `VALUE` — Numeric value, threshold, modifier, duration, cost, or probability.
- `FORMULA` — Calculation or mechanical relationship.
- `CONDITION` — Requirement or situation under which something applies.
- `RELATION` — Relationship between mechanics.
- `EVALUATION` — Result of measurement, simulation, testing, or comparison.
- `STRATEGY` — Conditional decision guidance supported by the source material.
- `META` — Time-, scenario-, event-, or environment-dependent information.
- `UNVERIFIED` — Source claim not sufficiently established for use as a confirmed fact.

### AI_USAGE_RULES

- Treat `FACT`, `VALUE`, `FORMULA`, `CONDITION`, and `RELATION` as core reference knowledge when their status is verified.
- Do not treat `EVALUATION`, `STRATEGY`, or `META` as universal facts.
- Preserve conditions attached to any evaluation or strategy.
- Do not convert personal preference into factual knowledge.
- Do not infer missing information from the source guides.
- If a source claim is uncertain or reverse-engineered, preserve its uncertainty.
- When two sources describe the same mechanic, use one canonical entry rather than repeating the explanations.

## 21. SOURCE_AND_VERIFICATION

### SOURCE_SCOPE

- Guide 1: support-card evaluation and simulation methodology.
- Guide 2: broad gameplay/reference information.
- Guide 3: race mechanics and reverse-engineered race-system information.
- Guide 4: practical race/event strategy and track-specific information.

### VERIFICATION_POLICY

- Source claims are not automatically treated as confirmed facts.
- Reverse-engineered mechanics retain their verification status.
- Simulation results retain their methodology and scope.
- Meta recommendations retain scenario/event/server/time scope.
- Conflicting claims remain explicitly marked until resolved.
- Personal opinion is excluded from the canonical knowledge base.

### SOURCE_TRACEABILITY

- Retain lightweight source references for important or uncertain claims.
- Do not duplicate source explanations when one canonical entry can represent the information.

---

## 22. KNOWLEDGE_MAINTENANCE

### UPDATE_RULES

- Update the canonical entry rather than adding duplicate explanations.
- Preserve conditions and scope when updating values or recommendations.
- Re-evaluate time-sensitive meta information when the relevant game environment changes.
- Do not silently promote an evaluation or recommendation to a FACT.

### CANONICAL_ENTRY_RULE

- One concept should have one canonical location.
- Cross-references should point to the canonical section rather than duplicate its contents.
